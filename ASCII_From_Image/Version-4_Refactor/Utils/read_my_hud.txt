 
How to use it
You can integrate the HUD with zero clutter inside video_player.py.

At the top of the file:

from utils.hud import PlaybackHUD


Then in VideoPlayer.play() before the loop:

hud = PlaybackHUD(enable_logging=True)


And inside the frame loop (right after print(rendered_output)):

hud.update(self.sync_offset, self.total_skipped)


Finally, after playback ends:

hud.summary()
